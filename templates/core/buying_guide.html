Condition transparency

What the condition labels mean

The label describes the product’s sales condition. The actual listing, photographs and written quotation remain the specific record for each item.

NewNew equipment

Presented as newly supplied by the manufacturer, distributor or authorised supply chain. Packaging and included accessories can vary by item.

RefurbishedPrepared for resale

Previously owned or previously deployed equipment that has been prepared for resale and clearly identified as refurbished.

UsedPre-owned equipment

Previously owned equipment sold in its stated condition. Review the actual photographs and request clarification about wear, battery, accessories and tested functions.

Before you decide

Check these six areas

Purpose and workload

State the software, schoolwork, business task, design work, gaming or everyday use the device must handle.

Specifications

Confirm processor generation, memory, storage, graphics, screen size, ports, operating system and upgrade options.

Actual photographs

Where actual-unit images are supplied, examine the screen, keyboard, casing, ports, charger and visible cosmetic condition.

Battery and accessories

For portable devices, ask what battery information is available and confirm the charger or other accessories included.

Availability

Stock changes. Confirm the exact configuration and availability before travelling, paying or relying on a quotation.

Warranty and returns

Coverage can differ by product and condition. Use the product description, quotation, invoice or receipt as the specific written terms.

A safer purchase process

From enquiry to after-sales

1

Share the need and budget

Describe the intended use, preferred specifications, number of units and realistic budget.

2

Compare suitable options

Review price, condition, performance, portability, upgrade potential and current availability.

3

Request written confirmation

For accountability, request a formal quotation showing the selected item, configuration, quantity and applicable terms.

4

Confirm collection or delivery

Agree on the collection point, delivery destination, timing and any additional delivery or setup charges.

5

Keep the documents

Retain the quotation, invoice, receipt and any product-specific support or warranty information.

Useful requests

Ask for the information that matters

  • Exact model and configuration
  • Current stock and final price
  • Actual images where available
  • Included charger and accessories
  • Product-specific warranty or return terms
  • Delivery, installation and setup charges
